Feature Engineering & Processing
Δx & Lag(1)

Percentage change and first-order lags computed to capture momentum and immediate trajectory.

μ(3m, 6m)

Rolling means over 3-month and 6-month windows to smooth noise and identify medium-term trends.

σ(Rolling)

Rolling volatility estimation to quantify localized market instability.

Z-Score Normalization

Indicators normalized via z-scores. Higher = Stress.

♧ Evaluation Protocol

Model validation strictly adheres to a chronological train/test separation to prevent data leakage and simulate realistic forecasting accuracy.
